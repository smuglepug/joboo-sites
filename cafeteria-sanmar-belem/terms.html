About this page

This is an unofficial demonstration prepared to show what a website for Cafeteria SanMar could look like. It is not yet the official site. Prices and hours shown here are illustrative.

How to order

Build your order from the menu and send it on WhatsApp to +55 91 3254 7685. We confirm everything by message before we start.

Prices

All prices are in R$ and may change. The price that applies is always the one we confirm on WhatsApp before preparing your order.

Delivery

We deliver around 456; Avenida João Paulo II. Outside that area the order is collected at the counter. Give your full address for delivery.

Catering and large orders

Catering and bulk orders need 48–72 hours' notice.

Privacy
