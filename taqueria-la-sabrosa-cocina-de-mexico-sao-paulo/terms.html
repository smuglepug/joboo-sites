This is an unofficial demonstration prepared to show what a website for Taquería La Sabrosa Cocina de México could look like. It is not yet the official site. Prices and hours shown here are illustrative.

How to order

Build your order from the menu and send it on WhatsApp to +55 11 2925 6189. We confirm everything by message before we start.

Prices

All prices are in R$ and may change. The price that applies is always the one we confirm on WhatsApp before preparing your order.

Delivery

We deliver around 246; Rua Francisco Leitão; São Paulo; Pinheiros. Outside that area the order is collected at the counter. Give your full address for delivery.

Catering and large orders

Catering and bulk orders need 48–72 hours' notice.
