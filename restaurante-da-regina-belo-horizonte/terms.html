About this page

This is an unofficial demonstration prepared to show what a website for Restaurante da Regina could look like. It is not yet the official site. Prices and hours shown here are illustrative.

How to order

Build your order from the menu and send it on WhatsApp to +55 31 3412-5944. We confirm everything by message before we start.

Prices

All prices are in R$ and may change. The price that applies is always the one we confirm on WhatsApp before preparing your order.

Delivery

We deliver around 894; Rua Icaraí. Outside that area the order is collected at the counter. Give your full address for delivery.

Catering and large orders

Catering and bulk orders need 48–72 hours' notice.

Where to find us

894; Rua Icaraí
Open daily · ask us for today’s hours

Privacy

This demonstration collects no personal data. No payment is processed here — the order is confirmed directly on WhatsApp.
